# Main Street Tycoon: prompts for the icon and covers

Prompts for an image generator (ChatGPT / GPT image, Ideogram, Midjourney,
Leonardo…). Each prompt below is complete: paste one block as it is.

## How to get the best result

1. **Attach the references** (the `reference/` pictures I sent you; they're
   kept out of git) to every generation, using your tool's image or character
   reference:
   - `hero-front.png` and `hero-three-quarter.png`: the hero, exactly as he
     looks in the game. Always attach at least one, so he doesn't change.
   - `map1` to `map4`: one picture of each town, so the places look like the
     game.
2. **Title text:** generators often misspell words. ChatGPT image and
   Ideogram are the most reliable for text. If the title comes out wrong, use
   the "no text" line at the end of each prompt, and the title can be added
   afterwards in the game's own font.
3. **Sizes Playgama asks for** (PNG or JPEG, up to 10 MB, exact pixels):

   | Cover | Size | Ratio to generate |
   |---|---|---|
   | Landscape | 1920 × 1080 | 16:9 |
   | Portrait | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
   | Square | 800 × 800 | 1:1 |

   Generate at the ratio, then resize to the exact size.
4. **For YouTube Playables**, keep a version with no text or logos: Google
   asks for none in thumbnails.

## Before uploading

- The hero looks like the reference: ginger hair, glasses, moustache, navy
  suit, top hat.
- The title reads exactly **MAIN STREET TYCOON** (Playgama checks that the
  title matches the game).
- Nothing appears that isn't in the game: no weapons, no other games'
  characters, no real brands.
- No odd AI artefacts: extra fingers, melted faces, garbled letters.
- Resized to the exact pixel sizes above.
- Viewed small (about 150 px wide): the hero and the title still read.
