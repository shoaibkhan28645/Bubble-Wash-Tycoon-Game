# 3D model credits

The buildings (`buildings/`), cars (`cars/`) and people (`people/`) are made
for Bubble Wash Town in Blender, from the scripts in `art/blender`.

The trees, plants and rocks in `nature/` are by **Kenney** (https://kenney.nl),
released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: free for any use, no
attribution required. Downloaded from Kenney's official OpenGameArt uploads.

| Folder | Pack | Version | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `nature/` | Nature Kit | 2.1 | https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit |

Only the models the game uses are copied here, unchanged.
