# 3D model credits

The buildings (`buildings/`) and cars (`cars/`) are made for Bubble Wash
Town in Blender, from the scripts in `art/blender`.

The people in `people/` and the trees, plants and rocks in `nature/` are by **Kenney** (https://kenney.nl),
released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: free for any use, no
attribution required. Downloaded from Kenney's official OpenGameArt uploads.

| Folder | Pack | Version | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `people/` | Mini Characters | 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/mini-character-1 |
| `nature/` | Nature Kit | 2.1 | https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit |

Only the models the game uses are copied here. The player
(`character-tycoon.glb`) and the staff (`character-*-staff.glb`) are Mini
Characters restyled in Blender (`art/blender/kenney_tycoon.py`,
`art/blender/kenney_staff.py`); the rest are unchanged.
